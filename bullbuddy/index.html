Good to know

Questions before you start.

Who is Bull Buddy for?

For anyone who trades or invests — complete beginners and active traders alike. Pick the markets you care about and the app adapts how much it explains to your experience.

Which markets does it cover?

Stocks and ETFs, indices, forex majors and crosses, gold, silver, oil and gas, crypto, US rates and the main macro gauges like the dollar index and VIX.

Does Bull Buddy give trading signals or advice?

No. Bull Buddy is educational. It explains what is happening and why it may matter, and helps you review your own process. Decisions are always yours.

Where do whale and liquidation alerts come from?

They are read live from public exchange streams (Binance, OKX, Bybit, Kraken and Coinbase) while the app is open, and only shown for your markets and watchlist.

Can I practice without real money?

Yes. Practice trading uses virtual money at live prices, and every closed practice trade lands in your journal so you can review it like a real one.

What happens to my data?

Your profile, watchlists, journal and learning progress are stored on your iPhone. When you use the AI features, your question and the relevant market data are sent to generate the answer.
