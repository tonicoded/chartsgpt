Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 12, 2026
Short versionCHAIN does not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal data. Your game progress and settings remain on your device.

Information CHAIN stores

CHAIN stores puzzle progress, CHAIN IQ, ranks, solve times, mistakes, hint use, streaks, remaining hearts, and app preferences locally on your device. This information is used only to provide the game and preserve your progress.

Information CHAIN collects

CHAIN does not require an account and does not collect personal information. The app does not use advertising, third-party analytics, tracking technologies, remote databases, or behavioral profiling.

Data sharing

CHAIN does not sell or share data with advertisers, data brokers, analytics providers, or other third parties. Apple may independently process App Store download, diagnostic, and device information under Apple's own privacy terms.

Network access and permissions

The core game works without sending your puzzle activity to a server. CHAIN does not request access to your contacts, photos, camera, microphone, precise location, or advertising identifier.

Deleting your data

You can reset saved game progress from the app's settings where available. Removing CHAIN from your device deletes the app's locally stored data. Because CHAIN has no accounts or remote profile database, there is no separate online account to delete.

Children

CHAIN is a general-audience puzzle game. It does not knowingly collect personal information from children or any other users.

Changes to this policy

If CHAIN's data practices change, this policy will be updated before the new practices take effect. The latest revision date appears above.
