Good to know

Questions before you start.

Does ChartsGPT provide buy or sell signals?
ChartsGPT shows informational analysis, possible setups and scenarios. It does not place trades or replace your own research and risk management.
What do I get from a chart screenshot?
A structured view of trend, market structure, key levels, possible scenarios and a trade plan with triggers and invalidation.
Can AI predict where price is going?
No. Markets are uncertain. ChartsGPT helps you evaluate possible paths and the levels that would change the idea.
Which markets and timeframes can I analyze?
You can review crypto, forex, stocks, indices and metals. Use a clear screenshot from the timeframe you trade and include enough history to show the wider structure.
What screenshot works best?
Include the symbol, timeframe, current candles and enough price history. Avoid overlays or panels that cover the chart.
Is ChartsGPT free?
ChartsGPT is free to download and includes free analyses. Full access requires a Pro subscription, which renews unless cancelled in your App Store settings.
Is ChartsGPT available on Android?
The Android version is in development. Join the beta waitlist from the Google Play badge above to hear when it is ready.
