AI chart analysis questions

Does ChartsGPT provide buy or sell signals?
ChartsGPT generates informational analysis and scenarios. It does not place trades and does not replace your own research, judgment, or risk management.
How is ChartsGPT different from using ChatGPT for charts?
ChatGPT is a general assistant that will describe a chart in prose. ChartsGPT returns the same structure every time: two to four key levels, a bull and a bear scenario, a trigger and an invalidation level, checked against live market data rather than read from pixels alone.
What is AI chart analysis?
Using AI to turn a chart screenshot into a structured plan: the levels that matter, two scenarios, the trigger that starts a trade and the price that proves it wrong. It produces clarity, not predictions.
Can AI predict where price is going?
No. Nothing predicts price reliably. ChartsGPT gives you structure and two scenarios so your decisions stay consistent, and it always states what would make each scenario wrong.
What screenshot gives the best chart analysis?
Use a clear image without panels covering the chart. Include the symbol, timeframe, current candles, and enough price history to show the wider structure.
Can ChartsGPT analyze forex and crypto charts?
Yes. ChartsGPT supports crypto, forex, stock, index, and metals charts for both day trading and swing trading workflows.
Which timeframe should I analyse?
The timeframe you trade, plus the one above it. The higher timeframe gives you direction and the lower one gives you the trigger.
Is ChartsGPT free?
ChartsGPT is free to download and includes free analyses. Full access requires a Pro subscription, which renews automatically unless cancelled in your App Store settings.
Do I need trading experience to use it?
No. The output is written to be readable without jargon, and every analysis names the level that would make the idea wrong, which is the part beginners most often skip.
