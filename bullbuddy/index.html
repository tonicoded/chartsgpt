Good to know

Questions before you start.

Who is Bull Buddy for?

For anyone who trades or invests — beginners and active traders alike. Pick your markets and the app adapts how much it explains with Simple and Pro.

Which markets does it cover?

Stocks and ETFs, indices, forex majors and crosses, gold, silver, oil and gas, crypto, US rates and the main macro gauges like the dollar index and VIX.

Does Bull Buddy give trading signals or advice?

No. Bull Buddy is educational. It explains what is happening and why it may matter. Decisions are always yours.

Where do the alerts come from?

Public sources: the economic calendar, SEC filings, Nasdaq, the CFTC, news publishers and the live streams of the big crypto exchanges. Only for your markets and watchlist, while the app is open.

Can I practice without real money?

Yes. Practice trading uses virtual money at live prices, and every closed practice trade lands in your journal.

What happens to my data?

Your profile, watchlists, journal and progress stay on your iPhone. When you use the AI features, your question and the relevant market data are sent to generate the answer.
