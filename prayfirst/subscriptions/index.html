plans and controlling price

Pray First may offer an auto-renewing weekly subscription and an auto-renewing yearly subscription. Product identifiers currently used by the app are com.anthony.prayfirst.weekly and com.anthony.prayfirst.yearly.

Prices vary by country, currency, taxes, and Apple pricing updates. The price, billing period, and offer shown in the app and Apple confirmation sheet immediately before purchase control. Website examples or earlier screenshots do not override Apple’s purchase sheet.

free trials and introductory offers

A three-day free trial may be shown with the yearly plan. It applies only if Apple shows the trial in the purchase confirmation and your Apple Account is eligible. Apple determines eligibility, which may be limited to new subscribers or one offer per subscription group.

If you accept a free trial, no subscription charge is due at confirmation; the subscription converts to the displayed paid yearly plan when the trial ends unless canceled. Cancel at least 24 hours before the trial ends if you do not want the paid renewal.

If Apple’s purchase sheet does not show a free trial, assume payment will be charged when you confirm.

payment and automatic renewal

  • Payment is processed by Apple and charged to your Apple Account at confirmation or when an accepted free trial ends.
  • The subscription automatically renews for the same displayed duration until canceled.
  • Apple may charge renewal within 24 hours before the current period ends.
  • Apple may apply taxes, price changes, billing retry, or a grace period under its rules and notices.
  • Deleting Pray First, revoking permissions, or deleting local data does not stop renewal.

manage or cancel

On iPhone or iPad, open Settings, tap your name, tap Subscriptions, choose Pray First, and tap Cancel Subscription. You may also manage Apple subscriptions at account.apple.com.

Cancellation stops future renewal and normally leaves paid access available until the end of the current paid period. If no Cancel button appears and Apple shows an expiry date, the subscription may already be canceled.

restore purchases

Use Restore Purchases on the Pray First paywall while signed in with the Apple Account used for the original purchase. Pray First uses RevenueCat to read Apple receipt and entitlement status. Restore does not create a new charge.

If restore finds no active plan, verify the Apple Account, check the subscription in iOS Settings, reopen the app, and try again. Then email support with the error message—never send a full payment card number.

refunds and billing disputes

Apple is the merchant and controls App Store billing and refund eligibility. Request a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com. Pray First cannot directly issue an App Store refund or change Apple’s decision.

access, expiration, and changes

Paid access is based on the active Apple/RevenueCat entitlement. Access may end when the subscription expires, Apple revokes or refunds the transaction, or billing cannot be recovered. If a subscription product or service is discontinued, existing subscribers will receive the access or notice required by Apple’s rules and applicable law.
